Rules

How to Play

Deal

Each player gets 4 cards. Four cards start face up on the table.

Capture

With A–10, you may take any number of numeric cards from the table if their total exactly matches the card you play. Ace = 1, so the capture total is never above 10. Example: 1 + 2 + 3 + 3 = 9, captured with a 9.

Face cards

Jacks, Queens and Kings capture only the same face card from the table.

Place a card

You may place a card face up on the table instead of capturing, even when a capture is available.

New deal

When both hands are empty, each player receives 4 fresh cards while the deck still has cards.

Final table

After the final hands are played, remaining table cards go to the player who made the last capture.
